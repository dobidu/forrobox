## READ THIS FIRST

You have no prior context. This document tells you everything.

**Project:** Forró Box — a VST3 instrument plugin for Brazilian forró percussion. Five-channel step
sequencer, four regional groove profiles, a `CACHAÇA` humanisation knob, three timbre characters,
MIDI drag-out. A high-fidelity HTML/CSS/JS prototype in the repo is the design specification; the
deliverable is a native JUCE plugin, not a port of the prototype's code.

**Core value:** Producers get authentic, human-feeling Brazilian forró percussion grooves inside
their DAW without hiring a percussionist or programming every hit by hand.

**The single most important thing to know:** this project's verification discipline is unusually
strict, and it is strict because it keeps paying. Every claim is measured, every measurement
instrument is self-tested against a known answer including a case it must reject, and every plan
ends with mutation testing (negative controls) run from a committed tree. Twelve measurements were
wrong before the code was in Phase 3, and five more in Phase 4 plan 01. Do not relax this.

## Mental Context — the approach, and what nearly went wrong

**The pattern that keeps repeating, and the reason for the discipline:** my measurements are wrong
before the code is. Five times in 04-01 alone:

1. `isTransparent()` is `alpha == 0`, so the check "carries alpha" asserted the opposite of what it
   read.
2. `checkEqual` on a `juce::uint8` streams as a character, so a genuine mismatch printed
   `expected ?, got `.
3. Saturation is the wrong instrument for "subtle" at brightness 0.21 — it reported 0.50 for a
   24/255 channel move. The spec's own mix weight is the right claim.
4. The strip-divider expectation was `--line` over `--panel`; the correct composite is over `--bg`,
   because strips do not paint beneath the gaps.
5. **`inkMass` is only valid over black.** `--panel` has brightness 0.118, so an empty strip region
   already scores ~300 and every threshold that detects text is one an empty region also clears — the
   head-row check was an assertion that could not fail. `contrastMass` exists because of this.

**And two defects the controls found that no test had:**

- Zeroing the tracking inside `drawTracked` passed all 1275 checks. `letterSpacingEm * heightPx` was
  written out twice and only the copy in `trackedWidth` was tested, so every label could have lost
  the letter-spacing the design calls "a big part of the look" while the width calculation still
  reserved room for it. Exactly the `dry = 1 - 0.5 * wet` shape from 03-03. Now one `trackingFor()`.
- `writeReferenceRenders` upscaled the 1x bitmap under a comment claiming that is what the editor's
  transform does. It is not — `Component::setTransform` is applied by the PARENT to the graphics
  context, so the chassis paints at target resolution. Fixing it introduced a worse bug (setting the
  transform on the component and calling `paintEntireComponent` directly ignores it, so the 2x render
  became a 1200×780 chassis in the corner of a 2400×1560 image) and the check "six PNGs written"
  passed throughout, being another assertion that could not fail. Each render's far corner is now
  asserted to be the footer.

**Also worth knowing:** `--verify` on the font build caught that the instanced files were not
byte-reproducible while the fetched ones were. Three bytes: `head.modified` and the checksum after
it. Assigning `head.modified` is not enough — `fontTools` recomputes it from the clock at compile
time unless `font.recalcTimestamp = False`.

**And:** `theme::mix` does NOT call `juce::Colour::interpolatedWith`, on purpose. That function
quantises the proportion itself to 8 bits and round-trips through premultiplied integers, which put
the anchor tint's largest channel move at 27/255 where the spec's 12% gives 24, and a 50% mix at 127
where a browser gives 128. `mix` interpolates in float and quantises once.
