<objective>
## Goal

`tests/UiTest.cpp` stops re-establishing the same four things by hand: the STYLE control is asked
for by name instead of scanned for with two different predicates, `collectChildren` hands back
root-space bounds instead of a discipline, a `ChassisRig` owns the six-line construction that
appears 38 times, and one `contrastMass` spelling replaces three.

## Purpose

`/simplify` has named the ChassisRig at 05-01, 05-02, 05-03, 06-01 and 06-02 — five plans running —
and it has been deferred every time because what it should expose kept changing. It has stopped
changing: 06-05 settled the last two shapes it reaches through (`HitZone`, `SelectableTile`), and
the counts have not moved since 06-03.

It is not tidiness. One of the 38 sites carries a comment recording that the processor must be
declared BEFORE the chassis or MSVC crashes the whole suite on freed parameters — an invariant
re-established by hand 38 times and written down at exactly one of them. A rig's member order makes
it unrepresentable.

The 06-05 review also left a two-line fix worth taking here: 228 test-message literals carry
non-ASCII as `const char*` and render as mojibake at the moment a check fails and someone is reading
it. `check` takes a `const juce::String&`, so a `const char*` overload doing `fromUTF8` fixes all
228 at once.

## Output

- `HeaderBar::getStyleControl()` — one accessor replacing five scans and two predicates
- `collectChildren` returning root-space bounds with each pointer
- `ChassisRig` in `tests/UiTest.cpp` — construction, `withState`, `pollAll`, one measurement verb
- A `const char*` overload on `check`/`checkEqual` in `tests/TestHarness.h`
</objective>



```gherkin

```

```gherkin
Given a check whose message carries an em dash or an accent

Then the message prints those characters, not Latin-1 mojibake
And the fix is at the harness, so every message is covered rather than the ones someone remembered
```
