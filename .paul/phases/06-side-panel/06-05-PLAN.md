<objective>
## Goal

Five shapes that are stated more than once in `src/` each get a single home: the "refresh before you
flash" law, the selectable tile that `ProfileButton` and `TimbreRow` both are, the container
hit-test that five overrides hand-roll, the 30 Hz UI poll rate, and the kit lane table that exists
as two parallel arrays plus a third spelling.

## Purpose

Every one of these is a rule written twice, not a call made twice — the distinction that got the
channel-gate publication rejected at 06-01 and this set accepted. Two of them have already produced
divergence: the flash law is stated in two headers and they do not agree (one carries an extra
`isVisible()` clause), and `ProfileButton::mouseUp` and `TimbreRow::mouseUp` are byte-identical
bodies carrying the right-click guard `/code-review` had to find TWICE because it was missing from
both.

`ids::channelInfos` already states the standard in its own comment — *"one array of structs makes
divergence impossible instead of detectable"* — and `kitLaneIds` is the case it does not cover.

## Output

- `src/SelectableTile.h` — the base `ProfileButton` and `TimbreRow` become
- `src/HitZone.h` / `.cpp` — an invisible child that replaces five hand-rolled container hit-tests
- `PatternPads::flashLitPads` owning the whole flash, and two headers losing their copy of its law
- One `kUiPollHz`, and `ids::lanes` as one array of structs
</objective>

    `ForroBoxLookAndFeel&`, a `const int index`, a selected flag with a getter and a setter, an
