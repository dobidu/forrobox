<objective>
## Goal
Fill the 56 px footer: `MASTER` and its 120 px fader, `LIMITER` with a gain-reduction meter reading
the real limiter, the `DRAG MIDI` call to action, and the `OUTPUT` segmented control. And take the
header out of `Chassis` first, so the footer lands beside it rather than inside it.

## Purpose
The footer is the last region of the chassis with nothing in it — Phase 4's goal is that the chassis
reads as the prototype, and a blank strip across the bottom does not. It is also the moment
`/simplify` named at 04-04's UNIFY: `Chassis` is 1354 lines and ~41% header-only, and the footer
would be a third build/paint/refresh/resize triad inside one class.

## Output
`HeaderBar` and `FooterBar` as their own components, a `GainReductionMeter` reading
`MixBus::takeGainReductionDb()`, a `DragMidiButton` stub, and a `Chassis` that owns the four region
rows and the strips.
</objective>

## AC-1: `Chassis` owns the regions; the header and footer own themselves

```gherkin
Given the chassis
Then the header's controls, poll and painting live in HeaderBar
And the footer's live in FooterBar
And Chassis owns the four region rectangles, the five strips, and nothing else of either

```

**A refactor that changes a pixel it cannot account for is not a refactor.** The split moves ~750
lines; the proof that it moved them rather than altered them is a rendered comparison, not a green
suite — the suite would also be green if the header shifted two pixels.

**Amended twice at APPLY, both times because the original wording was wrong:**

1. *`ui-renders/` is gitignored.* The plan called those PNGs "the committed reference images" and
   they are build artefacts that have never been committed. The before is produced by building the
   COMMITTED TREE in a throwaway directory and running its own suite — this project's negative-control
   pattern, and strictly stronger than trusting an artefact of unknown age.
2. *Absolute pixel-identity is not achievable with a real child component, and the difference is
   agreed.* Making HeaderBar a `juce::Component` changes two things at once: its paint is clipped to
   its bounds, and a child paints AFTER its parent rather than between two of the parent's regions.
   The measured cost is **22 pixels, dark theme only, max channel delta 5**: rows 72-82 of columns
   552 and 736, which are the two 1 px inter-strip gutters. `paintGlobalKnobGroup`'s
   `DropShadow (accent, 18)` is dark-only (css:209 gives the light theme its own shadow with no outer
   glow, which is why the light renders are byte-identical and why that confirms the diagnosis), and
   it used to bleed below the header where `paintMatrix`'s `--line` gap fill — alpha 0x17 — composited
   over it. Agreed with the user rather than accepted silently: the glow now stays inside the header,
   and the alternative was a split of the controls only, leaving the header's painting coupled to
   Chassis and forcing FooterBar into the same weaker shape.

   It also closes a latent bug. Gated by `paintIfVisible`, a repaint that touched only the header
   drew that glow onto matrix rows it was NOT repainting.

## AC-2: The GR meter reads the real limiter

```gherkin
Given the limiter is reducing gain
When the footer's meter is polled
Then its fill is proportional to the reduction in dB and grows RIGHT to LEFT
And with LIMITER off the meter reads empty, because the bypassed limiter reduces nothing
And the meter decays smoothly rather than flickering between polls
```

**`takeGainReductionDb()` is an `exchange`, so there can be exactly ONE reader.** `MixBus.cpp:171`
accumulates `max(previous, reduction)` per block and the read zeroes it — a peak-hold since the last
poll. Two readers would each see a fraction of the peaks and both would be wrong. The meter is that
reader; a test that calls it directly must not run against a live editor, and the decay must be the
UI's own, because every read starts from zero.
