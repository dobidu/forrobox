# Forró Box

**A VST3 instrument for Brazilian _forró_ percussion.**

</div>

---

## About

Forró Box is a drum machine for forró — the northeastern Brazilian dance music built on
**zabumba**, **triângulo** and **pandeiro**. Five instrument channels, four regional groove
profiles, per-step velocity and ghost notes, and a `CACHAÇA` control that makes a programmed
groove breathe like a played one.

It is free software under the **GPLv3**, and the source is public.
