## Goal
**The user's choice at 14-03 planning: hold the header's stated rule** ("every constant below has a
design source and every one is compared against it").

- **IN:** move these from `EffectOverlay.cpp`'s anonymous namespace into `namespace drunk` in
  `Effects.h`, unchanged in value:
  - `RadialWashLayer`;
  - `kRadialLayers` (2 rows × 7 fields, css:92-93);
  - `kLinearAccent`, `kLinearTopAlpha`, `kLinearEndAlpha` (css:95).

  Then:
  - `EffectOverlay.cpp` reads them as `drunk::`;
  - `verify-theme.py` reads them out of `Effects.h` and declares that file as its input, in place
    of the `.cpp`, through `gate_inputs`.
- **OUT:** `drunk::kSwayCommitDegrees` goes to `Chassis.h`, as a private `static constexpr` of
  `Chassis`, beside the sway it quantises.
  - It is a rendering cadence, explicitly not a design value.
  - `Chassis.h` is enrolled too, so its excuse moves with it, under its new qualified name. Nothing
    leaves the gate.
- **`verify-geometry.py`:** the moved table and constants sit in an enrolled header now, so each is
  either compared there or excused BY NAME as "compared against css:92-95 by verify-theme". That
  names the checker, so the coverage check stays honest.

## Purpose
- **ROADMAP Phase 14:** "`src/Effects.h`'s unit — hold its stated rule".
- **The 08-05 deferral:** the header was created to end a split where one feature's numbers were
  checked by two scripts in two files. Yet its first commit held a non-design value, and the wash's
  own gradient constants stayed in a `.cpp` that another script text-parses.
- **Measured at planning:** the alternative (a glob over `src/*.h`) would expose 104 more
  constants across 22 headers. The user declined it.
