<objective>
## Goal

Turning `CACHAÇA` up past 65 fades a warm wash over the chassis. Past 88 the chassis sways, the
readout turns orange, and the label becomes `♪ NO PONTO` with a slow pulse. The groove stays usable
at 100.

## Purpose

`PLANNING.md:566-573` specifies it, and `PLANNING.md:907` names it as a thing to go and look at:
*"turn `CACHAÇA` up past 88 to see the easter egg."* It is the last user-facing feature in the
plugin that the prototype has and this build does not.

`PLANNING.md:573` also states the constraint that makes it a design problem rather than a decoration:
*"Keep it tasteful — the groove is still usable at 100%; this is a reward, not a gimmick mode."*

## Output

- A chassis-wide effect layer that can post-process what is already drawn — the mechanism the
  Ciclotron™ treatment will reuse in 08-05
- `♪` drawn as a `Path`, because no embedded family carries U+266A
- The wash, the sway, the label swap and the pulse, each driven by one value
</objective>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: The effect layer, and what it really costs</name>
  <files>src/DrunkOverlay.h, src/DrunkOverlay.cpp, src/Chassis.h, src/Chassis.cpp, CMakeLists.txt, tests/UiTest.cpp</files>
  <action>
    Build the layer that can modify pixels the chassis has already drawn, because `juce::Graphics`
    has no blend modes and no filters — verified, not assumed.

    THE MECHANISM IS FORCED, and the cost with it. `Component::paintEntireComponent` is public but
    NOT virtual (`juce_Component.h:1185`), and an invisible component receives no mouse events — so
    the chassis cannot be "painted only by us". The layer therefore takes a
    `createComponentSnapshot`, post-processes it, and draws it over a chassis that has already
    painted itself normally. That is a DOUBLE paint.

    MEASURE IT BEFORE BUILDING ON IT. 08-03 measured a chassis paint at 4.43 ms and a 936,000-pixel
    `BitmapData` walk at 2.85 ms, so the arithmetic says ~11.7 ms against a 33 ms budget at 30 Hz.
    The user was quoted 7.3 ms at planning, from the same arithmetic minus the double paint — so the
    quoted figure was 60% low and this task's first job is to replace both numbers with a measured
    one. If it exceeds 16 ms — half the frame budget — STOP and report rather than shipping an
    easter egg that makes the editor stutter.

    USE `BitmapData`, not `getPixelAt`. `/simplify` measured the difference at 3.9x on exactly this
    size of image, and `tests/UiTest.cpp:460` is the standing example of the slow form.

    The wash is `PLANNING.md:567-569`'s three gradients composited with SCREEN — `1-(1-a)(1-b)` per
    channel, which is why it cannot be an alpha overlay: over the light theme an alpha overlay
    darkens where screen brightens. `--drunk` is `clamp((cachaça − 65) / 35, 0, 1)`, from
    `forrobox.css:89-96`.

    NOTHING WHEN OFF. Below 65 the layer must take no snapshot and run no pass — not "a cheap pass",
    none. AC-5 is measured, so a lazy early-out that still snapshots would be caught.
  </action>
  <verify>
    cmake --build build-linux --target ForroBoxTests -j8 && (unset DISPLAY; ./build-linux/ForroBoxTests)
    The measured per-frame cost with the wash ACTIVE and with it OFF, both reported in the SUMMARY.
    Checks: below 65 the render is pixel-identical to the feature-absent build; the wash is
    monotonic in cachaça; no pixel darkens.
    Then run `/code-review` on the diff.
  </verify>
  <done>AC-1 and AC-5</done>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: The sway, against the editor's own transform</name>
  <files>src/Chassis.h, src/Chassis.cpp, tests/UiTest.cpp</files>
  <action>
    `forrobox.css:117-122`: a 6 s ease-in-out sway of ±0.18° at or above 88.

    THE TRANSFORM IS THE RISK, and it is why this is its own task. 04-01 decided the chassis is a
    fixed 1200×780 child scaled by ONE transform on the parent
    (`PluginEditor::resized` sets `AffineTransform::scale`), and that decision is load-bearing for
    every geometry check in the suite. A rotation must COMPOSE with that scale, about the chassis's
    own centre, and must leave the scale mapping intact — a rotation applied to the parent, or
    multiplied in the wrong order, changes what 1200×780 maps to and silently moves every hit test.

    Prove the composition rather than eyeballing it: at 0° the rendered chassis must be identical to
    the unswayed one, and the editor's own scale claim (04-01's AC-3) must still hold at 1x, 1.5x
    and 2x with the sway at its extreme.

    TOLD ITS ELAPSED TIME, never reading a clock — `KitOverlay`, `AboutOverlay`,
    `GainReductionMeter` and `HitVisualiser` all carry this law and the reason is recorded in each:
    three 04-04 checks failed on MSVC's clock rather than on the code. Use the clamped
    `PollTimer::secondsSinceLastTick (max)` overload, which is what every other animation here uses.

    EASE-IN-OUT IS A CURVE, not a lerp. `Surface.h`'s `cubicBezierEase` already solves CSS control
    points and 07-02 hoisted it for exactly this reuse; `ease-in-out` is (0.42, 0, 0.58, 1).
  </action>
  <verify>
    cmake --build build-linux --target ForroBoxTests -j8 && (unset DISPLAY; ./build-linux/ForroBoxTests)
    python3 scripts/verify-geometry.py
    Checks: the rotation stays within ±0.18°; at phase 0 the render matches the unswayed chassis
    exactly; the editor's scale mapping is unchanged at all three sizes; advancing by 3 s reaches
    the half-cycle the 6 s period implies.
  </verify>

<boundaries>

## DO NOT CHANGE

- **The editor's single scale transform.** 04-01 decided the chassis is a fixed 1200×780 child scaled
  once by the parent, and every geometry check rests on it. The sway COMPOSES with that; it does not
  replace it and it does not move to the parent.
- **The type scale.** If `NO PONTO` does not fit where `CACHAÇA` does, report it.
- **`CACHAÇA`'s audio behaviour.** This plan is entirely visual. The humanisation engine, its jitter,
  its velocity variation and its ghost probabilities are Phase 3's and are not touched.
- **Everything 08-01 to 08-03 shipped.**
- The 46 parameter IDs.

## SCOPE LIMITS

- **No Ciclotron™ treatment.** It reuses this plan's effect layer and is 08-05. Building both here
  would put two visual treatments that fail differently in one plan — the split this project has
  made at 02-03, 04-04, 05-02, 06-04, 07-01 and 08-02.
- **No U+266A in any font.** The glyph is drawn. 08-03 settled that and the coverage gate continues
  to report the absence without failing on it.
- **No new animation infrastructure.** `PollTimer` and `cubicBezierEase` already exist and every
  other animation in this chassis uses them.

</boundaries>
