<objective>
## Goal
Build the bateria kit overlay — the panel that edits BB / CX / HH / TOM individually — and make the
sequencer's rows reflect state: isolate on a row-label click, and dimming for muted or soloed-out
channels.

## Purpose
This closes Phase 5, and it closes a loop 05-01 deliberately left open.

05-01 made the BATERIA row show `max(bb, cx, hh, tom)` and write **caixa alone** — `app.js:389`'s own
comment says why: *"collapsed row edits caixa (cx) — the backbeat; deep edits live in the kit view"*.
That asymmetry has been shipping for three plans with no kit view to defer to, so four of the eight
lanes have been unreachable. This is the kit view.

The row state is the other half of "the sequencer is legible", which is Phase 5's goal. A muted
channel still shows a bright row today, so the grid and the strips disagree about what is audible.

## Output
- `src/KitOverlay.{h,cpp}` — the panel, its backdrop, and four editable sub-rows
- The sub-dots row on the BATERIA strip becomes its trigger
- Row isolate and mute/solo dimming in `SequencerGrid`
- Reference renders with the overlay open
</objective>

## AC-1: The overlay is the prototype's, and covers the whole chassis

```gherkin
Given the sequencer
When the BATERIA strip's sub-dots row is clicked
Then a 620 px panel slides in from the right over a `--bg` 78% scrim
And the scrim covers the WHOLE chassis
And the panel carries `BATERIA · KIT` with BATERIA in the bateria accent, a 26 px close button, and
  the Portuguese sub-line
And clicking the scrim, or the close button, dismisses it
And every declared length is cross-checked against forrobox.css
```

**The backdrop's extent is a spec conflict, resolved the established way.** `PLANNING.md:519` says it
covers "the matrix + side panel area"; `app.js:37` appends the subview to `#fb-window` and
`css:554` is `position: absolute; inset: 0`. The design source wins over PLANNING's prose — the same
ruling 04-03 made for `.pad.beat`'s duplicate declaration.

**No backdrop blur, decided with the user.** `css:556`'s `backdrop-filter: blur(3px)` has no JUCE
equivalent short of capturing the region and blurring it. The `--bg` 78% scrim the same rule
specifies does the separation work on its own. Record the divergence where the scrim is painted, so
it reads as a decision rather than an omission.

## AC-2: Four sub-rows that edit four lanes, and the main row follows

```gherkin
Given the overlay open
Then four rows show BB / CX / HH / TOM with their full names — Bumbo, Caixa, Chimbal, Surdo
And each row's pads show that lane's stored velocities at the CURRENT step window
And clicking a pad toggles THAT lane, not caixa
And the main BATERIA row's pad for the same step updates to the new max of the four
And the pads are 26 px tall, which is the overlay's own height and not the grid's
```

**This is the payoff of 05-01's asymmetry.** The collapsed row writes caixa because the deep edits
live here; until now there was nowhere to defer to, so `bb`, `hh` and `tom` could be shown but never
edited.

## AC-3: The panel slides in, and the animation is told its elapsed time

```gherkin
Given the overlay opening
Then the panel moves from 24 px right and 0 opacity to its resting position over 200 ms
And the easing is `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1)` — css:565
And the animation is driven by elapsed time it is TOLD, never by reading a clock
And a test can drive it to any point without waiting
```

**Told, not measured** — `GainReductionMeter` and `HitVisualiser` both carry this and the reason:
three 04-04 checks failed on MSVC's clock rather than on the code. An animation is the first thing in
this plugin that would be tempting to test by sleeping, and it must not be.

## AC-4: Rows reflect what is audible, and what the user is focused on

```gherkin
Given a channel muted, or another channel soloed
Then that row's pads and label dim to 32% — PLANNING.md:589
And the dimming follows the SAME mute/solo resolution the engine renders with
Given a row label clicked
Then that row isolates: every other row dims to 32%, and audio is unaffected
And clicking it again clears the isolate
And only one row is isolated at a time — PLANNING.md:591-592
```

**Isolate is visual only and must stay that way.** `PLANNING.md:591` is explicit: *"without affecting
audio. It's a focus aid for editing."* A test must prove the audio is untouched, not just that the
pixels changed.

    whatever the implementation produces.

    (`PLANNING.md:589`). Read `resolveChannelSettings()`, the same resolution the engine renders
    with and the same one 05-02's visualisers use. Do not re-derive audibility in the UI.

    **Row isolate.** Clicking a row's label isolates it: every OTHER row dims to 32%. Clicking again
    clears it. One at a time (`PLANNING.md:591-592`). The label box is already reserved as
    `SequencerLayout::Row::label`.

    Isolate is VISUAL ONLY and nothing about it may reach the audio. Keep it in the grid — not in
    `State`, not in a parameter — so there is no path by which it could. It is also deliberately not
    persisted: it is a focus aid for the current edit, and a reopened project restoring someone's
    isolate would be surprising.

    When both apply — a muted row that is also isolated, or an isolated row while another is muted —
    the row is dim if EITHER says so. State that in the code, because "which wins" is the question a
    reader will have.

    Avoid: a second timer. Avoid: dimming by painting a translucent rectangle over the row — the pads
    are components and a scrim over them would also dim the playhead crossing that row.
