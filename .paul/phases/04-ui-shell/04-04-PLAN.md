<objective>
## Goal
Fill the 72 px header. The logo lockup, a BPM field that drags by its OWN law, `SYNC` / `÷2` / `×2`,
play and stop driving the processor's real transport, the two 54 px `SWING` and `CACHAÇA` knobs in
their recessed group, and the right cluster's preset cycler and `STYLE` segmented control.

## Purpose
Phase 4's goal is that the chassis reads as the prototype. It does not yet: the header is an empty
gradient, and it holds the two signature 54 px knobs — `PLANNING.md:377` calls them "the visual
centre of the header" and they are the Knob component's most important instance, at a size 04-02
proved but never placed.

## Output
Four new components (`Segmented`, `ValueScreen`, `BpmField`, `LogoMark`), two new `Button` variants,
one published host-tempo atomic, and a header whose every cluster is drawn and — where a parameter
exists behind it — live.
</objective>

## AC-1: The BPM field's law is its own — anchored, in BPM units

```gherkin
Given the BPM field at 120 and a press at its centre
When the pointer is dragged 40 px UP
Then the bpm parameter reads 140, because the law is 0.5 BPM per pixel from the press ANCHOR
And dragging back to the press point returns it to exactly 120
And one wheel notch moves it by exactly 1, whatever the range
And a double-click opens text entry, and junk text is rejected rather than clamping to 40
And the value is clamped to 40..300 at both ends
```

**Three controls, three laws, and this is the third.** The knob drags `(dy / 160) × range`
(`controls.js:130`), the fader takes the pointer's position within its track (`controls.js:236`),
and the BPM field moves `0.5 BPM/px` from a value captured at mouse-down (`app.js:138`). The wheel
is `Math.sign(deltaY)` — ±1 exactly, NOT the knob's `max(1, range/50)`. Unifying any two of these
would be wrong, and this project has now recorded that shape three times.

## AC-2: SYNC makes the BPM field read-only and show the HOST's tempo

```gherkin
Given SYNC is off
When the BPM field is dragged
Then the bpm parameter changes

Given SYNC is on
When the BPM field is dragged, scrolled or double-clicked
Then the bpm parameter does NOT change and no host gesture is opened
And the field displays the tempo the host reported, not the bpm parameter's value
And it is visibly read-only rather than silently inert
```

**The host tempo is not currently reachable from the UI.** `PluginProcessor.cpp:563` reads
`position->getBpm()` inside `processBlock` and uses it locally. Publishing it is one
`std::atomic<float>` stored relaxed once per block — no allocation, no lock — read by the editor's
timer. `PLANNING.md:400` and the stub table at `:838` both say the field becomes read-only under
SYNC; it is the one header behaviour the spec states twice.

```gherkin

```

**Second instance of 04-03's ellipse.** `radial-gradient(120% 160% at 50% -30%, ...)` (css:204) has
an origin OUTSIDE the box, which the pad's did not — so the argmax instrument cannot be reused
unchanged and the claim has to be made about falloff direction instead. Build it the way
`StepPad::paintLit` does: a circular `juce::ColourGradient` of radius `ry` handed to a
`juce::FillType` carrying the x-scale. `Graphics::addTransform` would stretch the group's border too.
